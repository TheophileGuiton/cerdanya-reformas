## Why us (the three differentiators)
1. **We already look after these houses.** Cerdanya Privé manages luxury chalets every week — we know how mountain houses age (humidity, frost, snow loads, heating) and what guests and owners expect.
2. **One contact, everything handled remotely.** Permits, architect, trades, furniture, cleaning, keys. Weekly photo report. Owners in Barcelona or Paris don't need to drive up.
3. **Renovated to live in — and to rent.** Optional continuity: after the works, Cerdanya Privé can manage the house as a premium rental. We design with that in mind (durable materials, storage, guest flow, wellness).

## Who it's for
- **Primary:** owners of second homes in La Cerdanya, 40–65, Barcelona families and French buyers (Toulouse, Perpignan, Montpellier, Paris), plus some international. Property €600k–3M. Little time, high expectations, need trust.
- **Secondary:** recent buyers who need a full renovation before moving in; owners preparing a house for premium rental; estate agents referring clients.
- **In 10 seconds they must feel:** taste, calm, competence, local roots. "These people will handle it, and it will be beautiful and solid."
- **They must find fast:** projects, services, how it works remotely, how to start (form / WhatsApp).

## Services (ideas — keep 6–8)

2. **Rehabilitación de casas de piedra y bordas** — stone houses, barns, casas pairals: keep the soul, add comfort.

- Calm, precise, warm. Talks about stone, wood, light, warmth, silence, views of the Cadí, coming home after skiing.
